# Lean coverage

This edition contains 265 Lean modules pinned to Lean 4.29.1. Every advertised module has a renewed, attributed review of its mathematical scope. Every public declaration type and hypothesis was compared with the attached elaborated export. Module entries and generated declarations are not counts of independent mathematical results.

The 2026-10-14 corrected source snapshot passed a new clean strict build of all 265 modules after 16 source-comment corrections in 12 modules. Its exhaustive declaration census, recursive axiom/dependency inspection, exact declaration-identity comparison and five fresh kernel replays also passed. The census accounts for all 21,064 serialized declarations, including private and generated auxiliaries, with none missing or unaccounted for. Current module exports bind their types and line locations to the corrected source hashes.

The renewed correspondence review is model-assisted. It preserves conditional suppliers and distinguishes actual-game theorems from abstract interfaces, local counterframes and implementation definitions. In particular, the SJS implementation does not have a proved all-row `gameF` dictionary, and the end-to-end assembly does not certify a concrete fast algorithm.

The fresh audit observed only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. It found no `sorryAx`, custom axiom, native trust axiom, unsafe project declaration or logical dependency on the 25 generated partial runtime helpers. Negative controls confirmed rejection of `sorry` and an undeclared custom-axiom dependency. The 2026-10-13 full audit and its 45-module documentation amendment are retained separately as historical evidence, without relabeling them as the renewed run.
