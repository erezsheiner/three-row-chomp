# Renewed correspondence review of the delivered Lean records

All 265 advertised Lean module records have a renewed, attributed scope review in `SEMANTIC-REVIEW.json`. No unresolved mismatch between a public mathematical claim and its actual quantified Lean interface was found. Sixteen source-comment corrections in twelve modules were required; these have been independently verified in the separate corrected candidate. The immutable delivered extraction retains the original wording and its recorded findings.

The review distinguishes actual-game theorems, abstract results, conditional reductions, finite certificates, counterframes, technical utilities and import-only modules. It does not count the 21,064 serialized declarations as independent research results. Private/generated auxiliaries inherit their compiler-checked types and dependencies; their presence does not create a new advertised mathematical claim.

The three reviewers read the actual sources and interfaces in complementary ranges: `proof_coverage_audit` reviewed sorted module indices 0–159, root reviewed 160–199, and `lean_publication_package` reviewed 200–264. Individual reasoning and source evidence are recorded for every module. Selected high-risk proof bodies received additional reads, including the actual-game foundation, future-freeze diagonal inequality and the abstract end-to-end assembly. This is model-assisted correspondence review, not external human peer review, novelty certification, or an independent handwritten reproof of every compiler-generated auxiliary.

The complete public display was checked separately for every module. All 21,064 elaborated declaration types match the catalogue and attached module exports exactly. Each public title, summary and hypothesis line is present, and all 265 downloadable sources agree byte-for-byte with the reviewed source files and catalogue hashes. The 3,486 source-mapped declarations are evidence locations, not a count of independent mathematical achievements. `PUBLIC-INTERFACE-COMPARISON.json` records all source and page hashes.

The main scope conclusions are:

* The formal game has the intended poison convention and three legal cut families. The noncircular completion construction supplies the canonical recurrence model. Root independently reviewed this bridge.
* The future-freeze theorem uses a no-repeat premise at model level; its actual-game specialization pays that premise.
* The SJS implementation has local MEX and initial-state results, but no all-row `gameF` dictionary. The separately proved OBUQ canonical reconstruction does not establish that missing SJS bridge.
* `EndToEndInvariantAssembly` is a conditional interface over arbitrary structures and operations. It does not establish a concrete implementation, an inhabited invariant or an unconditional runtime bound.
* RM2, UnitDip, chronology, owner-cutoff and spectrum suppliers remain explicit wherever used. Definitions of these properties, equivalences between them, and conditional `False` conclusions are not proofs that their premises are inhabited or globally established.
* Abstract arithmetic and source counterframes are not represented as realizable canonical-game configurations.

The necessary source corrections remove unsupported human-review provenance, qualify proposed laws and interface limitations, restore omitted domain hypotheses, and identify historical proof-status comments as historical. They do not alter a Lean definition, theorem or proof. `FINAL-COMMENT-AMENDMENT-REVIEW.json` independently verifies every exact replacement against the candidate and checks that text outside nested block comments is unchanged.
